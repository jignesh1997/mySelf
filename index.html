How I Build

Clean Architecture, MVVM & MVI

Every app I build is structured around Clean Architecture — strict layer boundaries that keep business logic independent of frameworks, UI and data sources. The dependency rule always points inward: outer layers know about inner ones, never the reverse.

On the presentation layer I reach for MVVM for most screens — ViewModels exposing immutable StateFlow to a reactive Compose UI. For complex, event-heavy flows (BLE connection lifecycles or multi-step forms), I switch to MVI: a single source of truth, unidirectional data flow, and reducers that make state transitions explicit and trivially testable.

This keeps modules independent, swappable and unit-testable — and makes onboarding new engineers fast.
